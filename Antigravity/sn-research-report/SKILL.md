# Research Report Structure & Architecture

## Overview

Guides the architecture and authoring of comprehensive research reports. Enforces structured synthesis, evidential integrity, and executive clarity across technical whitepapers, market overviews, and policy analyses.

## Template Selection Matrix

| Reading Task | Domain Fit | Core Sections |
|---|---|---|
| **Panoramic Landscape** | Market overview, industry scan | Executive Summary, Industry Architecture, Market Sizing, Key Drivers, Competitive Matrix, Outlook |
| **Comparative Analysis** | Tech evaluation, product benchmarking | Comparison Criteria, Direct Benchmark Table, Tradeoff Analysis, Architecture Fit, Recommendations |
| **Empirical Investigation** | Fact-checking, forensic due diligence | Investigation Hypothesis, Evidence Logs, Cross-Verification, Contradictions, Verifiable Findings |
| **Chronological Timeline** | Policy evolution, corporate history | Milestones, Phase Transitions, Causal Factors, Strategic Implications |

## Structural Standards

- **L0 Executive Summary**: 1-page condensed takeaways, key metrics, and strategic recommendations.
- **Section Hierarchy**: Clear numerical headings, structured bullet points, and data tables.
- **Citation Hygiene**: Every factual assertion must link to verifiable sources with zero unsupported claims.
